Small-engine motorcycles are a fun, affordable way into riding. Before you buy, it helps to understand the main types, what the law expects from you, and what to wear.

## Types of small-engine motorcycles

- **Mini bikes and kids' bikes** (around 50–110cc): small, low and designed for young or very new riders, usually off-road and under supervision.
- **Pit bikes** (around 110–150cc): compact off-road bikes for tracks and open ground.
- **Light road motorcycles and scooters** (around 125cc): built for road use and commuting, and must be registered and licensed.

## Licensing and registration

Rules on licences, registration, insurance and minimum ages are set by the General Department of Traffic and can change. Before riding on public roads:

1. Check the current motorcycle licence requirements with the traffic department or on the official government services platform.
2. Make sure the motorcycle is registered, insured and carries valid plates.
3. Remember that off-road bikes are generally **not** allowed on public roads.

Always ask the seller whether a model is approved for road use or intended for off-road use only.

## Essential safety gear

Never ride without:

- **A certified helmet** that fits snugly. This is the single most important piece of gear.
- **Gloves, boots and long, tough clothing** to protect your skin if you fall.
- **Eye protection**: a visor or goggles keep out dust and insects.

## Your first rides

- Start in a quiet, open space, away from traffic.
- Practise braking, clutch control and slow turns before going faster.
- Check tyres, brakes, chain and lights before every ride.
- Ride within your limits. Experience comes with time.

## Choosing your first motorcycle

Choose a bike you can comfortably touch the ground on and handle at low speed. Smaller and lighter is usually better for learning. And whatever you ride, start with a certified helmet: you'll find helmets and safety accessories in our store.
