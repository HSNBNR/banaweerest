The best bike is the one that fits how and where you ride. Before looking at brands or prices, answer one question honestly: **where will you ride most of the time?**

## Mountain bikes: built for rough ground

Mountain bikes have wide, knobby tyres, strong frames and suspension that soaks up rocks, roots and sand. They are the right choice if you plan to ride desert tracks, wadis or rocky mountain trails.

- **Hardtail** (front suspension only): lighter, simpler and great value. A good first mountain bike.
- **Full suspension** (front and rear): more control and comfort on steep, technical trails.
- Look for: wheel size (27.5" or 29"), reliable disc brakes and enough gears for climbing.

## Road bikes: built for speed and distance

Road bikes are light, with thin tyres and drop handlebars that put you in an efficient, aerodynamic position. They shine on smooth tarmac, long weekend rides and group rides.

- Best for: fitness, long distances and riders who want to go fast.
- Trade-off: less comfortable on rough surfaces and not designed for sand or dirt.

## Hybrid and city bikes: the all-rounder

A hybrid mixes the upright position of a mountain bike with the lighter, faster wheels of a road bike. If you ride in the city, to the park or along the corniche, and only sometimes on gravel, a hybrid is usually the most practical choice.

Frame size matters more than almost anything else. A bike that is too big or too small is uncomfortable and harder to control. Visit our showroom and we will measure you, set the saddle height and adjust the handlebars before you ride away.

1. Where will I ride: trail, road or city?
2. How often, and how far?
3. What is my budget, including a helmet, lights and a lock?
